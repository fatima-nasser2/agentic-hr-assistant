Fatinova Technologies Inc. — Remote Work PolicyDocument Version: 1.4
Effective Date: January 1, 2024
Last Reviewed: March 15, 2024
Owner: People Operations Team1. PurposeThis policy establishes guidelines for remote work arrangements at Fatinova Technologies Inc. Fatinova Technologies embraces flexible work as a core part of its culture, trusting employees to deliver results regardless of their physical location.2. ScopeThis policy applies to all full-time and part-time employees. Contractors may work remotely by default unless their contract specifies otherwise.3. Eligibility
All employees who have completed their 90-day probation period are eligible for remote work
Employees must have a satisfactory performance rating in their most recent review
Roles requiring physical presence (lab work, hardware, on-site client work) are exempt and will be communicated clearly at the time of hiring
4. Work Arrangements4.1 Hybrid Work

The default arrangement at Fatinova Technologies is hybrid — a minimum of 2 days per week in the office
Office days are coordinated at the team level and communicated by the direct manager at the start of each month
Employees may request to change their designated office days with 5 working days notice
4.2 Fully Remote Work

Employees may apply for a fully remote arrangement after 6 months of employment
Requests must be submitted to People Operations and require approval from the department head
Fully remote employees must be available for quarterly in-person team gatherings, travel costs covered by Fatinova Technologies
Fully remote arrangements are reviewed every 6 months
4.3 Temporary Remote Work

Employees may work fully remotely for up to 4 consecutive weeks without formal approval, subject to manager awareness
Temporary remote work exceeding 4 weeks requires written approval from People Operations
5. Equipment and Internet
Fatinova Technologies provides each remote employee with a standard equipment package: laptop, monitor, keyboard, and mouse
Employees receive a home office stipend of $500 upon starting remote work, for furniture or accessories
Employees are responsible for maintaining a stable internet connection with a minimum speed of 20 Mbps
Fatinova Technologies provides a monthly internet subsidy of $30 for fully remote employees
Equipment remains the property of Fatinova Technologies and must be returned upon termination of employment
6. Availability and Communication
Remote employees must be available during core hours: 10:00 AM – 4:00 PM in their local time zone
Employees must respond to messages within 2 hours during core hours
All remote employees must have their camera on during team meetings unless technically not possible
Status must be updated on the company communication platform (Slack) at the start and end of each working day
7. Security and Confidentiality
Employees must use the Fatinova Technologies VPN at all times when accessing company systems remotely
Work must not be conducted on public or unsecured Wi-Fi without VPN
Employees must ensure their workspace is private during calls involving confidential information
Any security incidents must be reported to the IT team immediately at security@Fatinova Technologies.com
8. Performance and Accountability
Remote employees are evaluated on outcomes and deliverables, not hours logged
Managers conduct bi-weekly check-ins with remote team members
Consistent underperformance in a remote arrangement may result in a return-to-office requirement
9. Policy ReviewThis policy is reviewed annually. Employees will be notified of changes via company email.For questions contact: people@fatinovatechnologies.com
