Fatinova Technologies Inc. — Leave Policy
Document Version: 2.1
Effective Date: January 1, 2024
Last Reviewed: March 15, 2024
Owner: People Operations Team

1. Purpose
This policy outlines the leave entitlements and procedures for all Fatinova Technologies Inc. employees. Fatinova Technologies is committed to supporting employee wellbeing while maintaining operational excellence across all teams.

2. Scope
This policy applies to all full-time and part-time employees of Fatinova Technologies Inc. Contractors and consultants are not covered under this policy unless explicitly stated in their contract.

3. Types of Leave
3.1 Annual Leave

Full-time employees are entitled to 21 working days of paid annual leave per calendar year
Part-time employees receive annual leave on a pro-rata basis
Leave accrues at a rate of 1.75 days per month
A maximum of 10 unused days may be carried over to the following year
Carried-over days must be used by March 31 of the following year or they are forfeited
Annual leave requests must be submitted at least 5 working days in advance via the HR portal
Leave during peak business periods (end of quarter) requires 15 working days notice and is subject to manager approval

3.2 Sick Leave

Employees are entitled to 10 paid sick days per calendar year
Sick leave does not carry over to the following year
For absences exceeding 3 consecutive days, a medical certificate from a licensed physician is required
Sick leave may not be taken immediately before or after annual leave without a medical certificate
Chronic illness cases are handled individually by the People Operations team in consultation with the employee

3.3 Parental Leave

Primary caregiver — 16 weeks fully paid parental leave
Secondary caregiver — 4 weeks fully paid parental leave
Employees must have completed 6 months of continuous employment to be eligible
Leave must commence within 4 weeks of the birth or adoption of a child
Employees are encouraged to submit parental leave requests at least 8 weeks in advance
Upon return, employees are guaranteed the same or an equivalent role

3.4 Bereavement Leave

Employees are entitled to the following paid bereavement leave:

5 working days for the loss of an immediate family member (spouse, child, parent, sibling)
3 working days for the loss of an extended family member (grandparent, in-law, aunt, uncle)
1 working day for the loss of a close friend or colleague


Additional unpaid leave may be granted at the manager's discretion
Bereavement leave does not affect annual leave balance

3.5 Marriage Leave

Employees are entitled to 5 paid working days upon marriage
This leave must be taken within 30 days of the marriage date
A marriage certificate must be submitted to the HR portal within 60 days

3.6 Study and Exam Leave

Employees pursuing approved professional qualifications are entitled to:

2 paid days per exam up to a maximum of 10 days per year


Approval must be obtained from the direct manager and People Operations at least 4 weeks in advance
Proof of enrollment and exam scheduling must be submitted to HR

3.7 Unpaid Leave

Employees may request unpaid leave for personal reasons after exhausting paid leave entitlements
Requests must be submitted at least 2 weeks in advance
Unpaid leave exceeding 4 weeks requires approval from the department head and People Operations
Benefits and seniority continue to accrue during unpaid leave of up to 4 weeks


4. Public Holidays

Fatinova Technologies observes all official public holidays in the country of employment
Employees required to work on a public holiday will receive a substitute day off to be taken within 30 days
A full list of public holidays is published annually by the People Operations team in January


5. Leave Request Procedure

Employee submits leave request via the Fatinova Technologies HR Portal at hr.fatinovatechnologies.com
Direct manager reviews and responds within 2 working days
Upon approval, the leave is automatically reflected in the employee's balance
For urgent or emergency leave, employees should notify their manager via phone or messaging before submitting the portal request
Falsification of leave requests or supporting documents is grounds for disciplinary action


6. Return from Leave

Employees returning from leave of 4 weeks or more will have a structured reintegration meeting with their manager within the first week
Any changes to role, team, or responsibilities during the employee's leave must be communicated before the return date
Employees returning from parental leave are entitled to a phased return option — 3 weeks at 60% capacity — upon request


7. Policy Violations
Misuse of leave, including but not limited to falsifying medical certificates or taking leave without approval, will result in disciplinary action up to and including termination of employment, in accordance with Fatinova Technologies's Disciplinary Policy.

8. Policy Review
This policy is reviewed annually by the People Operations team. Employees will be notified of any changes via company email and the HR portal.
