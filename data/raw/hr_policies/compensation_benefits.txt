Fatinova Technologies Inc. — Compensation and Benefits Policy
Document Version: 1.8
Effective Date: January 1, 2024
Last Reviewed: March 15, 2024
Owner: People Operations Team

1. Purpose
This policy describes Fatinova Technologies's approach to employee compensation and the benefits available to all eligible employees. Fatinova Technologies is committed to offering competitive, fair, and transparent compensation.

2. Scope
This policy applies to all full-time and part-time employees. Benefits eligibility for contractors is outlined separately in contractor agreements.

3. Compensation Philosophy

Fatinova Technologies benchmarks salaries against the 75th percentile of the market in each country of operation
Salaries are reviewed annually in January based on performance and market data
Pay equity audits are conducted every 12 months to identify and address gaps
Salary bands for each role and level are available to all employees on the HR portal


4. Salary Review Process

Annual salary reviews take effect on February 1 each year
Employees must have been employed for at least 6 months to be eligible for an annual review
Merit increases range from 0% to 12% based on performance rating:

Performance Rating   Increase Range  
Exceptional          8% – 12%
Exceeds Expectations 5% – 8%
Meets Expectations   2% – 5%
Needs Improvement    0%

Salary review outcomes are communicated by managers in February each year


5. Bonus Structure
5.1 Annual Performance Bonus

All employees are eligible for an annual performance bonus
Bonus target is 10% of annual base salary for individual contributors
Bonus target is 15% of annual base salary for managers and above
Bonuses are paid in March each year based on the previous year's performance

5.2 Spot Bonuses

Managers may award spot bonuses of up to $500 for exceptional contributions
Spot bonuses require approval from the department head
They are paid in the following month's payroll


6. Benefits
6.1 Health Insurance

Fatinova Technologies provides comprehensive health insurance for all full-time employees
Coverage begins on the first day of employment
Coverage includes: medical, dental, and vision
Employees may add dependents at a subsidized rate of 50% paid by Fatinova Technologies
Full details of the health plan are available on the HR portal

6.2 Retirement Plan

Fatinova Technologies contributes 5% of base salary to each employee's retirement plan
Employee contributions are matched up to an additional 3%
Employees are eligible to join the plan after 3 months of employment

6.3 Learning and Development Budget

Each employee receives an annual L&D budget of $1,500
This may be used for courses, conferences, books, or certifications
Requests are submitted via the HR portal and approved by the direct manager
Unused budget does not carry over to the following year

6.4 Wellness Benefit

Employees receive a $600 annual wellness stipend
This may be used for gym memberships, mental health apps, sports equipment, or wellness activities
Claims are submitted quarterly via the HR portal

6.5 Mental Health Support

Fatinova Technologies provides access to 6 free therapy or counseling sessions per year through a third-party provider
Sessions are completely confidential and not reported to Fatinova Technologies
Access details are available on the HR portal

6.6 Stock Options

All full-time employees receive a stock option grant upon joining
Options vest over 4 years with a 1-year cliff
Details of individual grants are outlined in the employment contract


7. Payroll

Employees are paid on the last working day of each month
Pay slips are available on the HR portal by the 25th of each month
Any payroll discrepancies must be reported to People Operations within 5 working days of receiving the pay slip


8. Policy Review
This policy is reviewed annually by People Operations and Finance.
