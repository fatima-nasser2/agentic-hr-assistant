NovaTech Inc. — Hiring Policy
Document Version: 2.0
Effective Date: January 1, 2024
Last Reviewed: March 15, 2024
Owner: People Operations Team

1. Purpose
This policy defines NovaTech's approach to attracting, evaluating, and selecting talent. NovaTech is committed to a hiring process that is fair, transparent, inclusive, and efficient.

2. Scope
This policy applies to all hiring managers, interviewers, and People Operations staff involved in recruiting at NovaTech Inc.

3. Principles

Merit-based selection — all hiring decisions are based on skills, experience, and potential
Inclusive hiring — we actively work to reduce bias at every stage of the process
Candidate experience — every candidate deserves timely, respectful communication
Confidentiality — candidate information is treated with strict confidentiality


4. The Hiring Process
4.1 Opening a Role

All new roles must be approved by the department head and Finance before posting
A Job Requirements Document (JRD) must be completed by the hiring manager before recruitment begins
The JRD includes: role title, level, responsibilities, required skills, salary band, and team structure

4.2 Job Posting

All roles are posted internally for 5 working days before external posting
Internal candidates who apply are guaranteed an interview if they meet the minimum requirements
External postings go live on the NovaTech careers page and approved job boards simultaneously

4.3 Application Review

People Operations screens all applications within 5 working days of the posting closing
Screening criteria are defined in advance and applied consistently to all candidates
All screened-out candidates receive a response within 10 working days

4.4 Interview Process
NovaTech uses a structured 4-stage interview process:
StageFormatOwnerStage 130-min screening callPeople OperationsStage 260-min technical or skills assessmentHiring ManagerStage 3Panel interview (2-3 interviewers)Team membersStage 430-min culture and values conversationDepartment Head

All interviewers must complete NovaTech's Interviewer Training before participating
Structured scorecards must be completed within 24 hours of each interview
No hiring decision may be made based on a single interviewer's opinion

4.5 Offer and Onboarding

Verbal offers are made by People Operations within 3 working days of the final interview
Written offer letters are issued within 2 working days of verbal acceptance
The standard notice period before start date is 2 weeks minimum
All new hires undergo a structured 30-60-90 day onboarding plan


5. Probation Period

All new employees serve a 90-day probation period
A formal review is conducted at day 45 and day 90
Either party may terminate employment during probation with 1 week's notice


6. Referrals

NovaTech operates an employee referral program
Employees receive a $1,000 referral bonus if their referred candidate is hired and completes 90 days
Referrals do not bypass the standard hiring process


7. Equal Opportunity
NovaTech is an equal opportunity employer. We do not discriminate on the basis of gender, age, nationality, religion, disability, or any other protected characteristic. Any violation of this principle must be reported to People Operations immediately.

8. Policy Review
This policy is reviewed annually by People Operations.
