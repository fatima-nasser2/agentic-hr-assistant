NovaTech Inc. — Code of Conduct
Document Version: 3.1
Effective Date: January 1, 2024
Last Reviewed: March 15, 2024
Owner: People Operations Team

1. Purpose
This Code of Conduct defines the standards of behavior expected of every NovaTech employee. It exists to ensure that NovaTech remains a safe, respectful, and high-performing workplace for everyone.

2. Scope
This policy applies to all employees, contractors, and anyone acting on behalf of NovaTech Inc., in any location and in any context — including offices, remote environments, client sites, and company events.

3. Core Principles
NovaTech expects every employee to demonstrate:

Integrity — act honestly and transparently in all situations
Respect — treat every person with dignity regardless of their role or background
Accountability — take ownership of your actions and their consequences
Collaboration — support teammates and contribute positively to team culture
Excellence — take pride in the quality of your work


4. Workplace Behavior
4.1 Respectful Workplace

Harassment, bullying, or intimidation of any kind will not be tolerated
This includes verbal, written, physical, or digital behavior
Offensive jokes, comments, or imagery — including those shared via messaging tools — are prohibited

4.2 Discrimination

NovaTech prohibits discrimination on the basis of gender, age, nationality, religion, disability, sexual orientation, or any other protected characteristic
Any discriminatory behavior must be reported immediately to People Operations

4.3 Conflict of Interest

Employees must disclose any personal or financial interest that could conflict with their duties at NovaTech
Outside employment must be disclosed to People Operations if it involves a competitor or could affect performance
Employees may not use NovaTech resources, relationships, or information for personal gain

4.4 Confidentiality

Employees must protect confidential company information at all times
This includes client data, financial information, product roadmaps, and personnel matters
Confidentiality obligations continue after the end of employment

4.5 Use of Company Resources

Company equipment, software, and systems are for professional use
Limited personal use is acceptable as long as it does not interfere with work or violate any policy
Employees may not install unauthorized software on company devices

4.6 Social Media

Employees must not share confidential company information on social media
Personal opinions expressed online must not be presented as NovaTech's official position
NovaTech's social media policy provides detailed guidance and is available on the HR portal


5. Health and Safety

NovaTech is committed to providing a safe working environment
Employees must report any safety concerns or incidents to their manager or the Facilities team immediately
Employees must not attend work under the influence of alcohol or controlled substances


6. Reporting Violations

Employees who witness or experience a violation of this Code must report it via:

Direct manager — for non-sensitive issues
People Operations — at people@novatech.com
Anonymous ethics hotline — available 24/7 at ethics.novatech.com


All reports are treated with strict confidentiality
NovaTech does not tolerate retaliation against anyone who reports a concern in good faith


7. Consequences of Violations
Violations of this Code will result in disciplinary action proportionate to the severity of the breach, up to and including termination of employment. Serious violations may also be referred to relevant legal authorities.

8. Acknowledgment
All employees are required to sign an acknowledgment of this Code of Conduct upon joining NovaTech and annually thereafter. Acknowledgments are stored in the HR portal.

9. Policy Review
This Code is reviewed annually by People Operations and the Legal team.
For questions contact: people@novatech.com
Ethics Hotline: ethics.novatech.com
