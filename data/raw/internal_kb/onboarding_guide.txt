NovaTech Inc. — New Employee Onboarding Guide

Welcome to NovaTech! This guide will help you get set up and productive
in your first 30 days.

Last Updated: January 2024
Maintained by: People Operations

BEFORE YOUR FIRST DAY

1. Check your NovaTech email — your IT setup instructions will be sent there
2. Complete your employment paperwork via the HR portal: hr.novatech.com
3. Review the Employee Handbook sent to your personal email
4. Download Slack and join with your NovaTech email
5. Your manager will reach out to schedule your Day 1 welcome call

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

WEEK 1 — ORIENTATION

Day 1:
- Welcome call with your manager (9:00 AM)
- IT setup session with the IT team (11:00 AM)
- Team lunch (in-office or virtual)
- Complete mandatory compliance training in the HR portal

Day 2-5:
- Meet your team members (your manager will schedule these)
- Complete role-specific onboarding tasks shared by your manager
- Set up all required tools (see IT Guidelines)
- Review company values, mission, and strategy documents

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

YOUR 30-60-90 DAY PLAN

First 30 Days — Learn:
- Understand the product and business
- Meet all direct team members and key stakeholders
- Complete all mandatory training
- Shadow colleagues and attend all team ceremonies

First 60 Days — Contribute:
- Take ownership of your first project or task
- Begin contributing to team goals
- Share your observations and ideas with your manager
- Complete your 60-day check-in with People Operations

First 90 Days — Drive:
- Operate independently in your role
- Set your OKRs for the next quarter
- Complete your 90-day review with your manager
- Identify areas for growth and development

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Q: When do I get my company laptop?
A: Your laptop will be couriered to your home address before Day 1,
   or available for pickup at the office on your first day.

Q: How do I submit expenses?
A: Use Expensify. All expenses must be submitted within 30 days.
   Expenses over $100 require manager approval before submission.

Q: Where do I find my payslips?
A: All payslips are available in the HR portal at hr.novatech.com
   under My Documents > Payslips.

Q: Who do I contact if I have an HR question?
A: Contact your HR Business Partner or email people@novatech.com.
   For urgent matters, use the HR portal's live chat feature.
